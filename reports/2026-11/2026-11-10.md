### 1. TS-GEAR: Taobao Shangou GEnerative Ad Retrieval

- **LLM Score**: 0
- **Keyword Score**: 14
- **Authors**: Guofeng Tong, Xu Zhang, Guangliang Yu, Haiqiang Wang, Guannan Cong, Xing Guo, Yang Zhou, Jingjing Duan, Shuang Li, Feng Shi, Hualei Liu
- **URL**: <http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12180v1>
- **Submitted**: 2026-11-09 10:30:44
- **Topic Keywords**: query, queries, rag, retrieval, commerce, e-commerce, rank, search
- **Reason**: LLM scoring failed.

#### Abstract
> On-demand delivery platforms such as DoorDash and Uber Eats impose a constraint absent in traditional e-commerce: merchants beyond the user's delivery range are invalid candidates rather than merely low-ranked. Generative retrieval has recently shown strong potential in large-scale retrieval systems. Adapting it to this setting, however, poses three challenges. First, existing semantic IDs encode geography only as a soft signal, so reachability can only rely on a non-differentiable post-generation hard truncation. Second, user preferences vary substantially across both regions and queries, so aggregating historical behaviors without conditioning on geography and the current query yields biased interest estimates. Third, the retrieved candidate set is evaluated by its collective value, yet autoregressive training scores each sequence independently, with no set-level signal to discourage redundant near-duplicates. To address the challenges, we propose TS-GEAR, a unified generative retrieval framework for on-demand delivery: (1) Geo-Semantic ID splits merchant identifiers into geographic prefixes and semantic suffixes, making reachability a prediction target rather than a post-hoc filter; (2) Query-Geo Target Attention aggregates historical behaviors using the real-time query and location as dual targets, while encoding each behavior's own location into its attention key; (3) GMPO (Group Marginal Policy Optimization) rewrites advantages using a deduplicated group reward and marginal advantage within GRPO, avoiding auxiliary diversity objectives. Deployed online with a lightweight 0.1B backbone, TS-GEAR serves over 150 million queries per day in the search advertising pipeline of Taobao Shangou, consistently outperforming state-of-the-art generative retrieval methods on both HitRate and geographic validity, and achieving 3.8% gains in ad impressions and 4.2% in ad revenue.

#### Abstract
> Enterprise text-to-SQL starts with finding the tables a question needs, which is hard when a schema spans several databases and hundreds of cryptic tables. We ask whether models trained separately on each customer's example questions, reading only table names, column names, and declared keys, improve table retrieval. NIFT-SQL (Necessity-Informed Fusion for Text-to-SQL) fuses hybrid search over a retriever fine-tuned on the customer's questions with a fine-tuned 560M cross-encoder that scores every table for whether the query needs it, through a logistic regression fitted on held-out questions. On BEAVER, LiveSQLBench, and LiveSQLBench-Large (244 to 971 tables; four splits, three trainings), NIFT-SQL places every reference table in the top 15 for 69.2%, 89.5%, and 85.6% of test questions, against 30.4%, 41.0%, and 21.8% for hybrid search and 61.1%, 81.7%, and 70.4% for the fine-tuned retriever with the same labels. A zero-shot 8B reranker needs no labels and, routed by database, shows no resolved complete@15 difference on two collections; adding its scores as two fusion features gives a high-accuracy mode that reaches 72.4%, 92.5%, and 88.6%, ahead of the validation-selected zero-shot reranker variant on two collections, with no resolved difference on the third. Rank fusion of the two trained parts, grouped by database, shows no resolved difference from the learned fusion. Scored once on a newer BEAVER release, NIFT-SQL beats search and leads the label-matched retriever at top 8 on the synthetic test, but trails it at top 15 on real questions.

#### Abstract
> As multimodal agents accumulate increasingly long interaction histories, effectively retrieving and utilizing past information becomes critical for long-term interaction. Existing memory systems typically rely on semantic retrieval over compressed memories and expose predefined representations, which may miss relevant memories and overlook heterogeneous evidence needs across queries. We introduce \textbf{\hivemem}, a multimodal memory framework that interleaves \textbf{\textsc{H}}or\textbf{\textsc{i}}zontal Memory Expansion with \textbf{\textsc{Ve}}rtical Evidence Composition. #### Abstract

#### Abstract
> Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards is increasingly used to improve language model reasoning, yet its effects on sequential decision procedures remain poorly understood. We study this problem in clinical multi-turn agents that must decide what information to request, when to stop gathering evidence, and which action to take. We introduce three controlled environments: medication refill triage, acute diagnostic work-up, and dosing titration, each with hidden clinical states, query costs, exact adaptive oracles, and deterministic outcome verifiers.
